Orbis

Every Google-owned site is isolated in its own Firefox Container, so Google cookies never touch the rest of your browsing.

Protection

Container appearance

Renaming updates the existing container in place; your cookies and logins stay intact.

Blocking Google trackers on other websites

Google code is embedded across much of the web. Even without cookies, loading it tells Google your IP address and which page you are reading. Blocking stops the request before it is sent.
