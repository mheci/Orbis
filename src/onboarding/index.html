What is Orbis?

Orbis creates a separate container orbit for Google – an isolated space where Google's cookies stay locked inside.

You stay signed into Google and it works normally, but other sites can't see your Google session, and Google can't see your other browsing.

🔒
Isolated orbit

Google in its own box, everything else outside. No cross-tracking.

⌨️

Fastest way to use it

One hotkey, zero clicks – feels native.

Ctrl + Shift + O New Orbis tab – Google in its own orbit
Ctrl + Shift + G Open Google in Orbis container

Change shortcuts: Add-ons Manager → Gear → Manage Extension Shortcuts

🖱️
Or use your mouse

Toolbar button shows if page is protected, right-click any link → Open in Orbis, or move tabs in/out.

🛡️

Blocks trackers automatically

Separating cookies is half the job. Orbis also blocks Google trackers on other sites.

📊 Analytics, Tag Manager Blocked
📢 DoubleClick ads Blocked
👥 Social widgets Blocked
🔤 Fonts, Maps, reCAPTCHA Allowed

Fonts, maps, embedded videos, reCAPTCHA and Sign-in with Google are left alone to avoid breaking sites. Strict mode in settings blocks more, but sign-in stays allowed so you never get locked out.

🚀

You're ready!

That's it – no complicated configuration needed. It just works.

Orbis automatically puts Google in its orbit, blocks trackers elsewhere, and never makes network requests of its own.

Tip: Extension is invisible in background – no telemetry, no tracking, everything stays on your device. Works great with uBlock Origin.
